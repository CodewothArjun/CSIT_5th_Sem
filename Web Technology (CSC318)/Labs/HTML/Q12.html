Amrit Science College (ASCOL)

Introduction

Amrit Science College, commonly known as ASCOL, is one of the oldest and most prestigious science colleges in Nepal. It is affiliated with Tribhuvan University and is located in Lainchaur, Kathmandu.

Academic Excellence

ASCOL offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in the field of science such as BSc CSIT, BSc Microbiology, and MSc Chemistry. It is known for producing academically strong and highly disciplined students.

Facilities and Environment

The college provides well-equipped laboratories, a peaceful library, and a student-friendly learning environment. ASCOL encourages students to be innovative and research-oriented.

Legacy and Reputation

Established in 1962, ASCOL has a long legacy of excellence in science education. Many renowned scientists and professionals in Nepal have graduated from this college.

Conclusion

ASCOL remains a top choice for science students in Nepal. As a proud student of ASCOL, I feel privileged to be a part of its glorious history.
