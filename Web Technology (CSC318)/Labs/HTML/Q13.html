Popular Places in Nepal

Pokhara

Pokhara is a beautiful city known for its lakes, mountains, and adventure tourism. It is the gateway to the Annapurna trekking circuit and offers serene views of the Himalayas.


Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is an important spiritual destination attracting pilgrims and tourists from around the world.


Bhaktapur Durbar Square

Bhaktapur Durbar Square is an ancient royal palace courtyard rich in Newari architecture and culture. It showcases traditional art, temples, and historical monuments.
