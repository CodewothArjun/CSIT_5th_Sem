Management Colleges in Nepal

Kathmandu College of Management is a reputed management college located in Lalitpur. It is known for producing business leaders in Nepal. Students often refer to BBA and MBA programs. The college was established in 1997, and is still one of the top choices for management education.
A quote from a student: Studying at KCM was a life-changing experience.

KCM emphasizes leadership and innovation in business education.

Engineering Colleges in Nepal

One of the top engineering colleges is Pulchowk Campus located in Lalitpur. It offers degrees in Civil, Computer, and Electrical engineering. Most students pursue BE degrees.
Pulchowk also offers MSc programs in advanced technologies.
The college was founded in 1972.
